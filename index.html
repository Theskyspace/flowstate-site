From the builder

I built this because transcripts aren't work.

I kept watching myself dictate a perfect paragraph, then spend four minutes turning it into a task, an email, and a doc. The talking was solved. Everything after it wasn't. So I started building the part that was missing.

It's still me and a short list of people who use it every day. No roadmap deck, no growth team — just the thing I wanted on my own Mac. If it ever feels loud or gets in your way, that's on me. Tell me and I'll fix it.
